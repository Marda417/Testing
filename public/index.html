    Where do I get the CSV?
    • ANZ Plus: in the ANZ Plus app, open your account → Statements / Transaction history → Export, choose CSV and a date range.
    • ANZ credit card: ANZ Internet Banking → select the card → Download transactions → CSV.
    • Amex: americanexpress.com.au → Statements & activity → Download → CSV.

    Re-importing overlapping statements is safe; duplicates are skipped. Amex exports purchases as positive numbers — that's detected automatically.
